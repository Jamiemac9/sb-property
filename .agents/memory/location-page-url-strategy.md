---
name: Location-page URL strategy
description: Why local SEO pages use paths on the primary domain rather than one subdomain per town.
---

Use crawlable `/areas/<town>` URLs on the primary domain for service-area landing pages rather than creating a separate subdomain for every town.

**Why:** Subfolders consolidate links, trust, content signals and crawl authority on the main business domain. Town subdomains act more like separate sites, add DNS and deployment overhead, and can encourage thin or duplicated local pages.

**How to apply:** Add each genuine service area to the shared area data, give it useful town-specific content, link it from the footer and related area pages, include it in the sitemap, and ensure the raw server response contains its H1, metadata and canonical URL.
